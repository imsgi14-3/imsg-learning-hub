## Quality rules used
1. The uploaded Chemistry Grade IX Assessment Framework/model paper is the assessment-design authority.
2. The uploaded Grade 9 Chemistry textbook is the content source.
3. Model-paper structure is used for format and distribution—not copied wording.
4. Straight and scenario questions are kept distinct.
5. Scenario groups are atomic and contain exactly four MCQs in this build.
6. Cognitive level is assigned from the task, not merely from wording.
7. Difficulty is independent of cognitive level.
8. Exact/near-duplicate cleanup is treated as a quality control, not as a license to inflate counts by option shuffling.
9. `concept_family_id` is included on authored expansions so the ExamEngine can later prevent over-selection from one underlying concept family if desired.
